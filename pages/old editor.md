#### National Anthem, Pledge and School Song

1. Students who are Singapore Citizens must sing the National Anthem and take the pledge. Students will take the Pledge with the right fist placed over the heart.
2. Students must stand at attention throughout the flag raising ceremony.
3. Students must sing the National Anthem and school song with pride.

#### Being Respectful
<b>We Respect Ourselves</b>

4. Students are to wear the prescribed school uniform and modification to the uniform is not allowed.
5. Students can wear their PE attire on days with CCA or PE/PAL lessons.

<img src="/images/OUR%20SCHOOL/School_rules.jpg" style="width:90%">

6. Usage  of sweater of jacket are only during cold weather or in air-conditioned rooms. The jacket or sweater should be plain and without loud motifs.

**We Respect One Another**

7.	Students are to respect one another at all times. There shall be no acts of bullying including verbal, physical and cyber bullying. Disciplinary action will be taken if students misbehave.
8.	Students are to respect the teachers at all times. Students are expected to greet teachers and follow instructions given.

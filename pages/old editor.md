<h4>Being Respectful</h4>
<b>We Respect Ourselves</b>

<ol start="4">
  <li>Students are to wear the prescribed school uniform and modification to the uniform is not allowed.</li>
  <li>Students can wear their PE attire on days with CCA or PE/PAL lessons.</li>
</ol>

<img src="/images/OUR%20SCHOOL/School_rules.jpg" style="width:90%">

<ol start="6">
  <li>Usage of sweater or jacket is only allowed during cold weather or in air-conditioned rooms. The jacket or sweater should be plain and without loud motifs.</li>
</ol>

<b>We Respect One Another</b>

<ol start="7">
  <li>Students are to respect one another at all times. There shall be no acts of bullying including verbal, physical and cyber bullying. Disciplinary action will be taken if students misbehave.</li>
  <li>Students are to respect the teachers at all times. Students are expected to greet teachers and follow instructions given.</li>
</ol>
